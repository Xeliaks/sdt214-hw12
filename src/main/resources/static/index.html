    Point Buy Rules (2024 PHB)

    • You have 27 points to spend.
    • Every ability starts at 8. Before bonuses, each one can be 8 to 15.
    • Costs: 8→0, 9→1, 10→2, 11→3, 12→4, 13→5, 14→7, 15→9.
    • Your background (not your species) gives the bonus. Its three listed abilities get either +2 to one and +1 to another, or +1 to all three.
    • Modifier = (total − 10) ÷ 2, rounded down.

    Species in the 2024 rules give no ability score bonuses, so this calculator doesn't ask for one.
